Independent recalculation instructions

Use actual_placed as the binary target and a model's *_probability column as its continuous score. Do not calculate ROC-AUC from thresholded predictions. AUC is the trapezoidal area under the ROC curve; average precision is the non-interpolated precision-recall average.


    

  

  
Interpret carefully: perfect holdout scores are unusually high. They show a stable pattern in these splits, not guaranteed real-world performance. Check target construction and derived features for leakage, and validate on a separately collected cohort before consequential use.
